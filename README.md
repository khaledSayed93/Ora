# ORA Water Features — Official Landing Page

A modern, responsive, and smart landing page for **ORA Water Features** (*Design · Build · Operate*), crafted with React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS from the Figma architectural design specifications.

---

## 🌊 Overview & Sections

1. **Navigation & Header (`Header.jsx`)**
   - Brand wordmark with exact Figma proportions
   - Smooth navigation links (`About ORA`, `Expertise`, `Services`, `Selected experience`, `Our approach`)
   - `Discuss your project ↗` consultation CTA
   - Mobile responsive menu drawer

2. **Hero Section (`Hero.jsx`)**
   - Immersive architectural lagoon photography
   - Multi-layer reading gradient scrim
   - Cormorant Garamond editorial serif typography (`Water, shaped by engineering.`)
   - Dual actions: `Explore our expertise ↗` and `Start a conversation ↗`
   - Sector badges: Residential · Hospitality · Commercial

3. **01 / About ORA (`AboutSection.jsx`)**
   - Editorial headline: `One partner. Every dimension of water.`
   - Strategic narrative connecting developers, architects, and consultants
   - Three Core Pillars:
     - `01 / One Responsible Partner`
     - `02 / Engineering-Led`
     - `03 / Long-Term Support`

4. **Founding Team in Figures (`FiguresBanner.jsx`)**
   - Dark navy banner (`#102c51`)
   - Experience across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and UAE
   - Key metrics: `12` Featured projects, `35,000+` m² water surface, `90+` Swimming pools, `3` Countries

5. **02 / Our Expertise (`ExpertiseSection.jsx`)**
   - 4 Primary capability cards with illustrative imagery:
     - Swimming Pools (Private, leisure, Olympic)
     - Lagoons (Swimmable & landscape with sand/regenerative filtration)
     - Fountains (Classic jets, musical & dancing with DMX choreography)
     - Aqua Parks (Water slides, lazy rivers)
   - 3 Specialist capabilities: Waterfalls & water walls, Spas & therapy pools, Water treatment systems

6. **03 / What We Do (`ServicesSection.jsx`)**
   - Complete lifecycle scope:
     - `01 Turnkey Contracting`
     - `02 Design & Consultancy`
     - `03 Operation & Maintenance`
     - `04 Renovation & Upgrading`
   - Interactive hover details and scope indicators

7. **04 / Selected Experience (`PortfolioSection.jsx` & `ProjectModal.jsx`)**
   - 4 Featured Projects with concept imagery and technical scale:
     - **Seashell Playa — Lagoons C2** (North Coast, Egypt · 5,200 m² water surface)
     - **Creek Town — Phase 01** (New Cairo–Suez Road, Egypt · 16,000 m² · 5 machine rooms)
     - **OUD Wellness Offices’ Lagoon** (Riyadh, Saudi Arabia · 2,820 m² lagoon · 11 pumps · 62 lights)
     - **MY ŌTEL North Coast** (El Dabaa, Egypt · 30+ pools · 12+ jacuzzis · 16+ fountains)
   - Interactive Case Study Modal for every project
   - Additional experience: Egyptian Flag Musical Dancing Fountain & JAZ Aquamarine Resort

8. **05 / Engineering First (`EngineeringSection.jsx`)**
   - Interactive Hydraulic System study drawing
   - Layered SVGs: Circulation pipe network, pump with rotating impeller, media bed filtration vessel, UV treatment unit, water level and wave surface
   - Core engineering principles: Calculated not assumed, BIM/MEP coordinated, DMX/PLC controlled

9. **06 / How We Work (`ProcessSection.jsx`)**
   - 6-Stage continuous delivery timeline:
     - `01 Concept & Feasibility`
     - `02 Detailed Design`
     - `03 Tender & Procurement`
     - `04 Construction & Installation`
     - `05 Testing & Commissioning`
     - `06 Operation & Maintenance`

10. **07 / Beyond Handover (`BeyondHandoverSection.jsx`)**
    - Pool edge visual detail
    - Aftercare scope: Preventive care, water treatment, system upgrades

11. **08 / A Collaborative Discipline (`PartnersSection.jsx`)**
    - Developers: Mountain View, IL CAZAR, Hassan Allam Properties, The Waterway Developments, TRAVCO
    - Consultants: DMA, ECG, SHAKER, POE Engineering Consultants, ACE Moharram.Bakhoum

12. **09 / Let’s Talk Water (`ContactSection.jsx`)**
    - Direct communication channels (Email, Phone, Website)
    - Interactive inquiry form with location, feature type, service type, stage, brief, and file attachment

13. **Brand Splash Screen (`SplashScreen.jsx`)**
    - High-fidelity recreation of Figma Frame `20:3`
    - Accessible via "Splash screen" button in the footer
